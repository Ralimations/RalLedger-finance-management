## Account lifecycle implementation notes

- Account editing is deliberately limited to name, institution, description, icon, color and dashboard inclusion. Account type, currency, opening balance, creation time, ledger balance and reconciliation metadata remain unchanged.
- Account deletion checks, in one Room transaction, for journal-entry and journal-line references, reconciliations, receivable payment destinations and subscriptions. If any are present, the account remains intact and can be archived instead.
- Account creation and archive/restore continue to use Room; a schema migration adds the description field. The encrypted backup serializer includes it and accepts earlier schema-2 backups with an empty description.
- Category edits and merges preserve journal rows and amounts; merge only changes category references and reparents subcategories in one Room transaction.
- Connected emulator instrumentation passes through schema 15 for account/category lifecycle operations, merge and history protection, receivable category reference integrity, backup/restore, and schema-1/schema-2 migration chains.
- Reconciliation is available from each active financial account's overflow menu. It records a balance correction as a journal adjustment, leaving the initial opening entry and prior transactions intact; asset and liability increases and decreases have repository-level coverage.
- Account-menu reconciliation passed emulator UI smoke with synthetic `UI_Test_Wallet` data. That account and its ₱125.50 adjustment remain on the development emulator; no actual user financial records were entered.
- Savings-goal allocations reserve cash already represented by account journal balances. They reduce Available Money but do not add to assets/net worth; the connected lifecycle test confirms balances and journal rows remain unchanged.
- Encrypted restore validates goal references and event-derived reserve amounts. Connected backup/restore coverage passes; manual goal-screen smoke remains pending because no emulator was online after the suite.
- Transaction correction is available only for income, expense and transfer journal entries. It validates all targets before writing and records the original reversal plus corrected replacement in one Room transaction. The original financial record is never overwritten.
- Duplicate copies transaction details into a new form; it creates no journal rows until the user saves the new entry. Transaction filter controls are available in the journal; date filtering uses a start-inclusive/end-exclusive range.
- The transaction correction and duplicate dialogs plus reversal result were visually checked on `emulator-5554` using synthetic data. Automated connected tests cover correction atomicity, journal preservation, invalid target rejection and receivable exclusion.
- Budget spending is aggregated from persisted expense entries and transfer fees for the transaction's local calendar month. Reversed original entries and reversal rows are excluded; corrected replacement entries count in their corrected period/category.
- A category cannot be permanently deleted while a budget refers to it. Category merges move non-colliding budgets and reject duplicate category/month plans before changing journal or category data.
- Budget records are included in encrypted backups with validation for category type, positive limit, valid month/year, unique IDs and unique category/month combinations. Connected budget CRUD, merge and backup coverage passed.
- Receivable edits change descriptive fields and due date without rewriting the recognition posting. Cancelling reverses recognition and is blocked until active collection/write-off entries are reversed. Write-offs create balanced expense/receivable journal lines; both partial and full write-offs remain in history and can be reversed.
- Receivable write-offs count toward the selected expense category in budgets and Insights. Schema-7 backup records write-off amount, category, journal ID, date, notes, and reversal state; validation reconciles outstanding balance against active collections and write-offs.
- Subscription archive state is included in encrypted backup/restore; schema versions before 6 default it to false. Metadata edits preserve last-payment, cancellation, and status fields, and deletion is blocked when last-payment metadata exists. Connected lifecycle and backup round-trip tests passed.
- Receivable archive state defaults to false for older backups and databases; the new write-off table is included in encrypted restore. The populated schema-1→8 and schema-2→8 migration chains, receivable lifecycle and backup tests passed on the emulator.
- Expected-income forecasts are excluded from actual income totals until converted; conversion follows current receivable recognition semantics and is visible as actual recognized income. A converted forecast cannot be edited or deleted, preventing divergence from its posted receivable.
- Populated schema-1 and schema-2 migration fixtures passed through schema 8 on the emulator. Expected-income lifecycle and encrypted restore also passed; manual checks of user-created records after app restart remain outstanding.
- Bills and loans are separate domain records linked to their recognition journal entries; payment events link to payment journals and cash accounts. Bill creation debits the selected expense category and credits the bill liability. Loan receipt debits a cash asset and credits loan liability without booking income.
- Repayments record principal against liability and interest against an expense category; payment reversals restore both the cash account and outstanding principal. The app blocks cancellation while active payments remain and only archives closed records. Amount and category edits are locked after posting to preserve the audit trail.
- Dashboard liability totals, cash-flow proceeds/outflows, Insights expense totals, budgets, category merge/delete guards and account deletion guards account for debt records. Encrypted backups accept older schema-8 files with empty debt arrays and validate new debt/payment journal links and outstanding-balance reconciliation.
- The first connected debt test caught a foreign-key ordering issue. Journal entries now post before debt rows within the same atomic transaction; focused debt coverage and all 14 connected tests pass.
- Manual emulator screen review caught and fixed a Bills & loans tab routing mismatch. The final APK showed the correct debt screen, Add bill and Add loan forms, and Subscriptions route. No records were entered; payment form interactions and restart persistence remain for hands-on QA.
- Interest credit estimates are forecasts only; actual interest is booked only when the user enters a bank-confirmed credit. Each actual posting records gross income, optional withholding-tax expense and net asset increase; reversal posts the exact inverse and retains its history.
- Account archival also archives its attached interest profile, and the account action message explains the linked change. Restoring the account does not silently reactivate the profile. Schema-11 migration, repository integration, backup round-trip and screen behavior still need connected verification; the latest emulator was offline.

## MyMoney design permission and asset scope

The project owner reports receiving permission from the MyMoney owner to use MyMoney assets and design principles. The permission terms and permitted assets are not present in this repository. RalLedger therefore applies general usability principles and retains its own identity; it does not bundle MyMoney artwork, source code or trademarks. Before using specific third-party assets, record the actual approved scope and distribution limits.

## Dashboard widget preference notes

- Six financial summaries can be shown or hidden independently and reordered with adjacent up/down controls. Available Money remains the first default metric, with Net Worth separate.
- Widget order and visibility use local DataStore preferences. Unknown or duplicate saved order entries are normalized; missing widgets are appended so app updates retain the full widget set.
- These preferences only change dashboard presentation. Financial totals continue to come from the existing persisted records and accounting calculations.
